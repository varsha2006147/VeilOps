How VeilOps works

The cloud sees the shape of your operations, never the secrets.

A collector runs inside your network. It keeps raw logs on your machines, removes secrets, replaces names with codes and sends only a few numbers. These four instruments are drawn live from that data: every plate, block and tower is something real.
